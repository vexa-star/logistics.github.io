Design direction:
A maritime/industrial visual identity using deep navy, port-signal orange, container-yard geometry, maritime data labels, port photography and condensed industrial typography. Responsive desktop and mobile layouts are included.

Photography:
The page uses remote Unsplash image URLs in CSS. For a production launch, replace these with institution-approved port / container terminal photography and ensure the image licences and usage rights are cleared.

Logo:
The current header/footer logo is a text-based placeholder. Replace it with the official EM Normandie logo asset before publishing.

Enquiry form:
The six-step enquiry form intentionally mirrors the structure and interaction pattern of the supplied International Marketing landing page: name, email, mobile, preferred intake, location, date of birth, preferred contact method and consent. It is a front-end demonstration only and does not send, store or transmit applicant data. Connect it to EM Normandie's official admissions CRM/form endpoint and insert approved privacy wording before launch.

Content:
Programme facts and admissions summary are based on the official programme page. Confirm all details with Admissions before publishing, as requirements and programme information may change.
